A taut monsoon thriller whose second half earns its interval twist. Trim the middle and it is ready to pitch.

A mid-budget, single-location thriller with a clear commercial hook and a marriage story underneath it. The first half builds dread with real economy; the second sags for ten pages before the ferry sequence pays everything off. Two roles need strong actors, and the rest can be cast from rising talent.
