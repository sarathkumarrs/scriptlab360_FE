Your script stays yours.

There is no Scriptlab360 account and no copy of your script on our servers.

  1. 01

    On your device

    Scripts, drafts and reports are saved in your browser, on your own computer or phone.

  2. 02

    Out only when you ask

    A report sends the script to the AI model you chose, for that one reading. Scriptlab360 never keeps it and never trains on it.

  3. 03

    Delete means gone

    Delete a script and its drafts and reports go with it.

Two tools send text out while you use them, and Scriptlab360 stores neither: writing in an Indian language sends each word to Google's input tools to convert it, and Speak sends your recording to the transcription model you chose. Whether a model provider keeps what it receives depends on that provider's terms.
